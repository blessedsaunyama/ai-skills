## Discussion questions (kids 8–12)
1. What did Daniel do when he knew the law was signed? (Dan 6:10: he prayed "three times a day ... as he did aforetime.")
2. Why couldn't King Darius save Daniel? (Dan 6:15: "no decree nor statute which the king establisheth may be changed.")
3. Who shut the lions' mouths? (Dan 6:22: "My God hath sent his angel.")
4. Why was no hurt found on Daniel? (Dan 6:23: "because he believed in his God.")
5. When is it hard for you to keep doing what is right? How can you remember that God is with you?

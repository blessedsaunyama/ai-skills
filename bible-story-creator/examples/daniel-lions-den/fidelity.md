## Accuracy notes
- **Left out on purpose (audience: kids 8–12):** v. 24, where the accusers and their families were cast to the lions. It's part of the account, but it's violent and isn't needed for this short video. Mention it in the lesson or discussion only with older audiences.
- **Simplified:** v. 7's "ask a petition of any God or man" → "pray only to the king"; v. 14's "sore displeased with himself" → "sorry"; v. 23's "no manner of hurt" → "no hurt".
- **Not stated in the text:** how many lions; what the angel looked like; whether Daniel slept; the den's size; Daniel's exact age.
- **Translation:** KJV throughout. Quotes keep KJV spelling ("hath", "thee", "lions'").
- **Myth check:** no "young Daniel", no lions doing tricks or being friendly pets (they're simply restrained: "have not hurt me"), and no extra dialogue for Daniel.

Daniel would not stop praying, not even when the king's law said he must.
King Darius gave Daniel a high place, because "an excellent spirit was in him."
Jealous leaders searched for a fault, but they found none.
So they asked the king to sign a law. For thirty days, pray only to the king, or be thrown to the lions.
Daniel knew the law was signed. He went home, opened his windows toward Jerusalem, and prayed three times a day, just as before.
The leaders caught him praying. The king was sorry, but his law could not be changed.
Daniel was cast into the den of lions. A stone was laid upon the mouth of the den.
That night, the king could not sleep.
Very early, he hurried to the den and cried, "O Daniel, servant of the living God, is thy God, whom thou servest continually, able to deliver thee from the lions?"
Daniel answered, "My God hath sent his angel, and hath shut the lions' mouths."
No hurt was found upon him, "because he believed in his God."
Daniel stayed faithful, and God was with him. He is with us too.
"He delivereth and rescueth." Daniel chapter six, verse twenty-seven.

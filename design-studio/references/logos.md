# Logo checklist (before showing logos)

`tools/logo.py` builds logos only from basic geometry (rounded squares, circles, hexagons,
rounded bars, arcs) and OFL fonts. Still check each concept:

## Must not resemble
- Famous marks: swoosh / tick, bitten apple, three stripes, interlocking rings, shell, golden arches,
  bird silhouettes, play-button triangle in a red rounded box, chat bubble with a phone (WhatsApp),
  "f" in a square, camera outline in a gradient square, the colour-split four squares, etc.
- **Church / denomination emblems** (e.g. the Seventh-day Adventist flame-and-open-Bible emblem, crosses
  combined with flames or doves in the same arrangement as an official emblem). For church work use the
  official logo file from the church; only make logos for local groups/ministries, and keep them clearly different.
- Government seals, flags as logos, university crests.

## Must be
- Readable at 32 px (icon-only version) and in one colour (mono version).
- Original initials / name spelling confirmed by the user.
- Delivered as SVG (master) + PNG; colour, mono, reversed, icon-only.

## Tell the user
"These are original, but I can't search every registered trademark. Before printing on a large scale or
registering it, do a quick reverse-image search (Google Lens / TinEye) and check your trademark office."
